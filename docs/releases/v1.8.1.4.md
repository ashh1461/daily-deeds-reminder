# Wird 1.8.1.4: the adhan can always be stopped, and the place can be changed from the adhan settings (pre-release)

A hotfix to 1.8.1.3. It installs over 1.8.1.3 and earlier 1.8.x builds (same signing key, version code 17). It carries out the "v1.8.1.4 hotfix" part of `docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md`.

## Stopping the adhan
Before this release the only way to stop a playing adhan was the small «إيقاف» button on the expanded notification. Now:
- **Swiping the notification away stops the adhan.** (Android 14 lets you swipe a foreground notification; before, the audio kept playing with no notification left.)
- **Tapping the notification stops the adhan** and opens the app.
- **The power button or either volume key stops it**, unless «إيقاف الأذان بزر التشغيل أو الصوت» is switched off in «إعدادات الأذان» (on by default). Android reports the power button only as "the screen turned off", which a screen timeout also causes, so screen-off stops the adhan only when you are clearly at the phone: the screen was already on when the adhan began, you unlocked it, or you turned it on yourself. A screen lit only by the adhan notification can time out without cutting the adhan. When the screen is off, press power twice (on, then off) or use a volume key.
- **A banner at the top of every screen** («يُشغَّل الأذان الآن» with «إيقاف الأذان») while the adhan plays.
- **No silent-but-unstoppable adhan:** when notifications are denied, or the «الأذان» channel is blocked, the adhan is not played (there would be nothing to press); the prayer-time reminder is shown instead.

## Other adhan fixes
- **During a phone call the adhan no longer plays over the call.** The audio-focus refusal was ignored before; now the prayer notification is shown instead.
- **A snooze follows the prayer's current mode.** A prayer switched to «تذكير» after snoozing no longer plays the full adhan when the snooze ends.
- **Fallback to the phone's sound** now also happens when the bundled adhan file cannot be opened at all (the flag was set too late).

## Changing the place («another area»)
- **The place card is now at the top of «إعدادات الأذان وطريقة الحساب»** as well as «أوقات الصلاة»: suggested cities, search, «موقعي» and typed coordinates.
- **«موقعي» asks for a fresh location** (up to 10 seconds) instead of only the phone's last known one, which was often empty. If location services are off, it offers to open the phone's location settings. The place is labelled after the nearest city, for example «موقعي الحالي (قرب صور)».
- **Typed coordinates use the time zone of the nearest city** (within 500 km), not the phone's, so coordinates in another country give that country's times. They are labelled «إحداثيات مخصصة (قرب …)».
- **The coordinates dialog accepts Arabic-Indic digits and «٫»** (٣٣٫٢٧), a comma, and any minus sign, and says why an entry is invalid instead of silently keeping «اعتماد» disabled.
- **The location buttons wrap** onto a second line at large font sizes instead of being cut off.
- **The time rows use the place's date**, not the phone's, so a place in another time zone shows the right day.

## Blessed nights on the right evening
The Islamic day starts at sunset, so Laylat al-Qadr (19, 21, 23 Ramadan) and the night of mid-Sha'ban are the evenings *before* those dates. They were announced on the morning after. Now they are listed (Today, widget, calendar, morning note) on the civil date of their eve, marked «(تبدأ بمغرب هذا اليوم)». The strike (19th) and martyrdom (21st) of Amir al-Mu'minin and the birth of Imam al-Mahdi (15 Sha'ban) stay on their own days, now as separate entries.

## New section: «أعمال الأشهر القمرية»
A new entry in «العبادات» opens the deeds of each Hijri month from Mafatih al-Jinan («الباب الثاني: في أعمال أشهر السنة»), complete, as in the book:
- **The current Hijri month first** (following your sighting adjustment in the calendar), then «أعمال عامة لكل الشهور» (the introduction and the deeds common to every month), then all twelve months from Muharram to Dhu al-Hijja.
- Each row opens the full section in the Mafatih reader (font size, favourites and search work as usual). Ramadan includes Du'a al-Iftitah, Du'a Abi Hamza al-Thumali and the Sahar du'as, which the book places in that chapter.
- Al-Qummi covers Rabi' al-Akhir, Jumada al-Ula and Jumada al-Akhira in one chapter, so those three months share it.

## Occasions added (dates from Mafatih al-Jinan)
- 7 Dhu al-Hijja: martyrdom of Imam Muhammad al-Baqir (ع).
- 7 Safar: birth of Imam Musa al-Kazim (ع), at Abwa.
- 15 Dhu al-Hijja: birth of Imam Ali al-Hadi (ع).
- 3 Safar: birth of Imam al-Baqir (ع), which al-Qummi gives as his own view; the existing 1 Rajab entry is kept and now reads «على قول», as the book lists it as another opinion.

## Compatibility notes
- **Backups:** a backup made with 1.8.1.4 restores fully into 1.8.1.4 and later. Restored into 1.8.1.3 or earlier, the general adhan settings (vibrate, silent mode, snooze length) fall back to their defaults, because the older versions do not know the new setting; everything else restores.
- **«موقعي» keeps the phone's time zone** (the phone is at that place, so its clock is the local one); only typed coordinates take the zone of the nearest city.

## Verification
- New unit tests: coordinate parsing (Arabic-Indic and Persian digits, «٫», minus signs, error messages), nearest-city zone and labels (Tyre, Najaf, London, Qom, open sea), the night occasions on their eve, and the saved adhan settings with the new hardware-stop flag (settings saved by older versions keep working, with the flag on).
- Full unit test run, Android lint and the debug build: see the build result recorded in `progress.md`.

## Not verified (needs a person with a phone)
On Android 13 and 14, with an adhan playing, each of these should silence it within about a second:
1. swiping the notification away;
2. tapping the notification (the app should also open);
3. the power button (screen on), and pressing it twice when the screen was off;
4. volume up and volume down, with the screen on and with it off;
5. «إيقاف الأذان» in the in-app banner;
6. with notifications denied for Wird: at prayer time there should be no adhan audio at all.

Also: an incoming call at prayer time (no adhan over the call); «موقعي» indoors and with location services off; the coordinates dialog with an Arabic keyboard; the place card at font scale 1.3.

Not in this release (planned for v1.8.2 or later, see `docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md`): a high-latitude rule, an evening reminder before Maghrib on blessed nights, saved places, restoring alarms before the first unlock after a reboot, and the targetSdk 35 upgrade.
