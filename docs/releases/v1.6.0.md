# Daily Deeds Reminder 1.6.0

A real adhan, configurable prayer times, and offline city search.

## Adhan
- **Four modes per prayer:** إيقاف · صامت · إشعار · أذان (full audio). Installs from earlier versions keep their old on/off choice as «إشعار», so nobody is switched to audio unexpectedly.
- **Full adhan playback** through a foreground media service on the alarm stream, with a Stop and a Snooze button on the notification, a maximum duration, audio focus, and an option to stay quiet while the phone is on silent or vibrate. If Android refuses to start the service, a normal notification is posted instead.
- **Pre-adhan reminder** (5 / 10 / 15 / 30 minutes before) and a **manual ±15 minute adjustment** for each prayer.
- **Test button** for the selected voice.
- **Late-alarm guard:** an alarm delivered more than 10 minutes late (Doze, inexact alarms) no longer announces "now".
- New notification channels (adhan, reminder, silent) created once at app start instead of on every notification.

## Voices
- **My own file:** pick any audio file you have the right to use; it is copied into the app's private storage after checking size, type and that it decodes.
- **Downloadable voice packs:** the app can list and download voices published in `voices/manifest.json`. Every voice must carry a licence and a permission note, a size under 25 MB and a SHA-256, or it is not shown; the downloader is HTTPS-only, restricted to GitHub hosts and verifies the checksum. The catalogue is empty for now: the famous Shia adhans have no free licence, so none is published until written permission is recorded. See `docs/voices/README.md`.

## Prayer times
- **Calculation methods:** Leva Research Institute (Qom) 16° / 4° / 14° (default), University of Tehran 17.7° / 4.5° / 14°, or custom angles; optional Maghrib delay in minutes after sunset; Imsak time (default 10 minutes before Fajr), shown on the prayer screen. Tehran results match an independent reference within 2 minutes in five cities.
- **City search** over 34,000 cities (GeoNames, Arabic and Latin names, works offline), manual latitude/longitude entry, the 28 suggested cities and "my location".
- The prayer screen and the Today tab now follow your method and adjustments, refresh every 30 seconds and show a live countdown.

## Security
This release adds the INTERNET permission (voice packs only). See `docs/SECURITY.md`: a unit test pins the exact permission list, only one class may touch the network, cleartext is disabled, and the dependency scan (93 packages) found no advisories.

## Known limits
- Not tested on a device or emulator: alarm delivery in Doze, the foreground-service start on Android 14+, audio focus, and the Stop/Snooze buttons need a real phone.
- Without the «المنبّهات والتذكيرات» permission Android may delay alarms by minutes; the app shows a warning card with a button to the right settings page.
- City names in Arabic exist for about a quarter of the list; others are searched in Latin letters.
