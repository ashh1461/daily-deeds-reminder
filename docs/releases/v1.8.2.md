# Wird 1.8.2: Android 16 target, alarms that survive a restart, high-latitude prayer times (pre-release)

Builds on 1.8.1.6 and installs over it and earlier 1.8.x builds (same signing key, version code 20). It is the platform release planned in `docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md` section 7.5.

## Platform
- **Targets Android 16 (API 36)**, as Google Play requires for new uploads. The minimum stays Android 8.0.
- **Edge to edge:** the app draws behind the status and navigation bars, as Android 15+ requires. The top bars, the bottom tabs and every screen keep their content clear of the bars and of camera cutouts in landscape.
- **Toolchain:** Android Gradle Plugin 8.13.2, Gradle 8.13, Kotlin 2.2.21 with the Compose compiler plugin, Compose BOM 2025.08.00, and current AndroidX libraries. This replaces Dependabot PRs #5–#9, which would have moved to AGP 9.
- **A dark start window in dark mode**, so a cold start no longer flashes white. It follows the phone's dark mode; with the in-app theme set against the phone's, the first frame still follows the phone.

## Alarms
- **Prayer alarms come back right after a restart, before the first unlock.** A phone that restarts overnight (for example for an update) no longer misses the Fajr adhan. To make this possible, the app's settings now live in Android's device-protected storage, which can be read before the first unlock. It is still encrypted, with the device key instead of your PIN. The settings move there once, the first time the app runs after the update. Downloaded or imported adhan voices stay in normal storage: before the first unlock the bundled adhan plays in their place.
- **A snooze survives a restart.** A snooze whose time passed while the phone was off is dropped.
- **An adhan is not played twice when the clock moves back** (the end of summer time, or a manual correction of up to three hours).
- **A short interruption pauses the adhan** (a call, a navigation prompt) and it resumes afterwards. The time limit keeps running.
- **On silent or vibrate**, when the adhan follows the ringer, the app now shows the prayer notification (and vibrates, if chosen) without briefly starting the adhan service.
- **The adhan is set as an alarm clock**, the kind of alarm Android is least likely to delay. The next adhan shows in the status bar and in the clock's "next alarm".

## Prayer times
- **High latitudes:** above about 48° in summer the sun does not sink far enough for Fajr (16°) or Isha (14°), and those times used to be missing, with no alarm. They are now estimated by the angle-based rule: a portion of the night (angle ÷ 60) before sunrise or after sunset. Estimated times are marked «تقديري» on the prayer-times screen, with a note. Times that can be computed are unchanged. Polar days with no sunset still show no time.

## Compatibility
- Settings, favourites and backups carry over unchanged. Backups are the same format.
- Downgrading to 1.8.1.x after 1.8.2 has run would start with default settings (the older version looks in the old place). Restore a backup if you need to go back.

## Verification
- Unit tests (new):
  - the high-latitude rule: London and Oslo at midsummer, unchanged times elsewhere, polar day
  - the repeat guard and its three-hour window
  - saving and restoring snoozes
  - the direct-boot decisions: when to move the settings, and what to do before unlock
- An instrumented test of the settings move (`PrefsStorageInstrumentedTest`) is compiled; it needs a phone or a working emulator to run.
- Full unit test run, Android lint and the debug build: see `progress.md`.

## Not verified (needs a person with a phone)
1. Install over 1.8.1.6: every setting, the place, favourites and reading positions are still there.
2. Restart the phone and do not unlock it before the next prayer: the adhan plays (the bundled voice if a downloaded voice was chosen). After unlocking, the chosen voice plays again for the following prayers.
3. Snooze an adhan, restart the phone before the snooze time: the snooze still plays.
4. Edge to edge on Android 15 or 16, in light and dark mode, portrait and landscape: nothing is hidden under the status bar, the navigation bar or a camera cutout, on the tabs, the readers, the mushaf and the settings.
5. A call during the adhan: the adhan pauses and resumes after the call.
6. Phone on silent with "follow the ringer" on: no adhan notification flashes; the prayer notification shows.
7. The status bar shows the alarm icon before an adhan, and the clock app shows it as the next alarm.
8. Set the place to London or Oslo in June: Fajr (and in Oslo Isha) show «تقديري», and their alarms fire.
9. Dark mode: a cold start shows a dark window, not a white flash.
