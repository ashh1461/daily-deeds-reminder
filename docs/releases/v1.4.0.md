# Daily Deeds Reminder 1.4.0

Complete texts, the full Sahifa Sajjadiyya, and a new emerald-and-gold look.

## Content
- **Every dua and ziyarah in full:** the abridged samples and their "check the original book" notices are gone. The Mafatih tab is now built from the complete book, split into named sections you can open directly: دعاء كميل, السمات, الصباح, العشرات, المشلول, يستشير, المجير, الجوشن الكبير والصغير, التوسل, الفرج, الافتتاح, أبو حمزة, السحر, المناجاة الخمس عشرة (each on its own), زيارة عاشوراء, الأربعين, أمين الله, الجامعة, زيارات الأئمة والمراقد, أعمال كل شهر، وغيرها: 130 sections, every paragraph of the book, ending with the author's closing note.
- **الصحيفة السجادية الكاملة:** all 54 supplications of Imam Zayn al-Abidin (peace be upon him) plus the supplements printed with it (6 additional supplications, the seven weekday supplications, and the fifteen Munajat), 82 sections, searchable and bookmarkable.
- **Weekday duas and ziyarat** (Home tab) already matched the book; the Saturday ziyarah now includes the book's second form and the author's note. Source and "excerpt" captions were removed from these screens.
- **Picture references removed:** wording such as "from the attached picture" is gone from the deeds and settings screens; real citations (e.g. Bihar al-Anwar) are kept.
- Fixed a conversion bug in the previous Mafatih text where the word before each page break was glued to the next word.

## Look and feel
- New palette: deep emerald and antique gold on warm parchment (light) or green-black (dark), an Islamic star-lattice banner on Home and each surah, ornamental dividers, rounded gold-hairline cards.
- Typography: Amiri (naskh) for the Quran, supplications and ziyarat; Tajawal for the interface. Both are SIL OFL licensed.
- Home hero shows the Hijri date, today's occasion and the next prayer time.

## Notes and limits
- The Mafatih book and Sahifa texts come from the OpenITI corpus (MIT) and carry **no tashkeel** (the digitization has none); they have not been proofread against a printed edition. The Quran text keeps full Uthmani marks.
- The previous curated, diacritized samples (Kumayl, Tawassul, etc.) were replaced by the full undiacritized sections; items that exist only in later add-on editions (Ale Yasin, Ahd) are not in this recension of the book.
- Favorites saved on the Mafatih book in 1.2.2/1.3.0 refer to the old section numbering and will not reappear; Quran and weekday favorites are unaffected.
- Interactive checks on a device were not possible in the build environment; visual styling is untested on-screen.
