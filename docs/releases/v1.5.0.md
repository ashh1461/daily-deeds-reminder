# Daily Deeds Reminder 1.5.0

A full re-organisation of the app's sections. No content was removed; everything now has one clear home.

## New structure
Five tabs, each with the same top bar (Search, Favorites, Settings):

| Tab | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **اليوم** | Hijri date and today's occasion, a live countdown to the next prayer, today's prayer times, today's dua and ziyarah, "continue where you stopped" (Quran / Mafatih / Sahifa) and then the daily deeds with their category chips. |
| **العبادات** | Prayer times and adhan alerts, qibla, calendar and occasions, weekday duas and ziyarat. |
| **القرآن** | The complete Quran. |
| **المفاتيح** | Mafatih al-Jinan only: الأدعية · الزيارات · المناجاة · التعقيبات · أعمال الجمعة · أعمال الشهور · الفهرس. |
| **الصحيفة** | al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya on its own: supplications 1-54 in groups of ten, the supplements, the seven weekday supplications and the fifteen Munajat, with its own search and "continue reading". |

## What changed in the Mafatih tab
- **الفهرس** is now a real table of contents grouped by Bab and Fasl, replacing the old "الكتاب كاملاً" chip that repeated 128 of its 130 items.
- Friday deeds have their own chip (they used to sit under "أعمال الشهور", which now only holds the months).
- The seven weekday duas and the weekday ziyarat have a single home (their screens, reachable from اليوم, العبادات and a pinned card in الأدعية / الزيارات); the duplicate rows were removed from the themed chips but remain in the الفهرس, so the book is still complete.
- Every chapter header shows its Bab and Fasl; lists that belong to a single chapter have no headers.

## Search, settings and fixes
- Search is a full-screen page opened from the top bar, with separate filters for Quran, Mafatih, Sahifa, weekday duas/ziyarat, Tafsir summaries and the daily deeds (deeds are now searchable).
- One grouped Settings page: prayer and adhan, calendar, deed reminders, appearance (system / light / dark) and credits. The unused "interactive sounds" switch was removed.
- The "reset today" action moved into the deed list's menu and now asks for confirmation.
- Fixed: a dead "١١٤ سورة" button; the prayer screen no longer goes stale (it refreshes every 30 s and at midnight); the notification permission is asked once instead of on every launch; a warning card explains how to allow notifications and exact alarms when they are blocked (without exact alarms the prayer alerts can arrive minutes late).
- Favorites saved on the Sahifa before this release keep working.

## Verification
