# Wird 1.8.4: the features still missing — resume tile, a full day of qada, blessed-night eves, adhan fade-in (pre-release)

Builds on 1.8.3 and installs over it and earlier 1.8.x builds (same signing key, version code 22). It holds the small features of `docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md` section 7.4 that were still open, the ideas of section 3 (the 7.2 IDEA row) apart from saved places, and the end of the backup-import work begun in 1.8.3.

## Today
- **The resume tile names the page.** In pages mode, «تابع من حيث توقفت» now reads «القرآن: سورة الكهف، صفحة ٣٠٢» and opens that page. In the list of ayahs it reads «القرآن: سورة الكهف، الآية ٤٥» and opens the list at that ayah (see below). When the last page belongs to another surah (the surah was opened from the list since), or nothing was read past the first ayah, it shows the surah only, as before.
- **A notification tap opens the Today tab.** Tapping any reminder, occasion or eve notification (or the playing adhan's notification, which also stops it) while the app is open on another tab or a pushed screen now goes back to «اليوم». Before, it only changed the deed category, out of sight. On a fresh start the app opens on Today as always.

## The Quran
- **The ayah list reopens at the last ayah**, not only at the surah. When you stop scrolling the list, the ayah at the top is saved; the resume tile reopens the same surah there. Opening a surah does not overwrite it with the first ayah, and opening another surah (from the list or the mushaf) starts that surah at its top. The saved ayah is part of a backup (`pref_last_ayah`).

## Make-up prayers (qada)
- **«قضيتُ يوماً كاملاً»**: a new card at the top of the qada screen marks one of each of the five daily prayers as made up in one tap. It is enabled only while all five are still owed. The five changes share one batch, so one «تراجع عن الأخير» reverts the whole day; a full day made right after «إضافة صلوات الأيام» is its own batch, so the undo never reverts both. The fast is not touched.

## Blessed nights
- **An evening reminder before blessed nights (opt-in).** A new switch in «التقويم والمناسبات», «تذكير مسائي قبل الليالي المباركة», sends a notification on the eve: every Thursday for ليلة الجمعة, and the day before ليلة النصف من شعبان and the three Laylat al-Qadr nights (the nights of the calendar). It comes an hour before Maghrib at your place (with your Maghrib adjustment), or at 17:00 when Maghrib cannot be computed that day. The notification names the night(s) and says the night begins at this evening's Maghrib. It follows the Hijri offset and moves at once when you change the place, the Hijri offset, the calculation method or the Maghrib adjustment; it is rescheduled after a reboot; a reminder delivered more than three hours late (the phone was off) is dropped rather than shown after the night began; and is part of a backup (`pref_blessed_eve_enabled`). Off by default.

## Adhan
- **Fade-in (opt-in).** A new switch in the adhan settings, «رفع صوت الأذان تدريجياً», starts the adhan quietly (5% of the player volume) and raises it to full over 10 seconds, along a curve that sounds like an even rise. The phone's alarm volume is never changed. It applies to the test button too. Off by default; adhan settings saved by earlier versions read it as off.

## Backup import
- **No restart is needed any more.** After an import, every screen reads the imported values again: since 1.8.3 the tasbih, qada, khums, eclipse and Imsak switches, reader sizes and fit mode; now also the favourites, the place, the Hijri offset, each prayer's alarm, the adhan and prayer settings, the occasion and eve reminders, the theme, the last surah, ayah, page, Mafatih and Sahifa sections, the pages mode, the daily deeds, the reminders on the Today tab and haptics. The alarms are set again from the imported values, as before. The message is now just «تم استيراد N عنصر.», and the «أعد تشغيل التطبيق» dialog is gone.

## Widget
- **A preview in the widget picker.** On Android 12 and later the picker shows the widget itself with sample text (Beirut, the next prayer, a Hijri date, ليلة الجمعة); on Android 8 to 11 it shows a drawing of the widget's card. Before, the picker showed the app icon.

## Compatibility
- Settings, favourites and backups carry over unchanged. Two new backup keys (`pref_last_ayah`, an integer, and `pref_blessed_eve_enabled`, a switch) are accepted by this version; older versions skip them when they read a newer backup, as they skip any key they do not know. The adhan settings gain a sixth field; older settings strings (four or five fields) are still read.

## Verification
- Unit tests (new):
  - the resume tile's text in pages and list mode, the ayah from the list's top item, and which ayah the reader reopens (`QuranResumeTest`)
  - the full day of qada: one of each prayer, the fast untouched, one undo for all five, its own batch after added days, only when all five are owed, saved and loaded (`QadaFullDayTest`)
  - the blessed-night eves: every Thursday, the eve of mid-Sha'ban, an hour before Maghrib or 17:00, today before the time, the next eve after it, the notification text (`BlessedEvesTest`)
  - the fade-in curve (start, end, rising, below the straight line) and the new adhan setting (off by default, round trip, five-field strings read as off) (`AdhanFadeTest`)
  - a view model hears only the imports after it was created, and the two new keys are backed up with their types (`DataReloadsTest`)
- Full unit test run, Android lint, the debug build and the instrumented test APK: see `progress.md`.

## Not verified (needs a person with a phone)
1. Install over 1.8.3: settings, the place, favourites and reading positions are still there; the new switches are off.
2. Pages mode: read the mushaf to page 302 and go back to Today; the tile reads «القرآن: سورة الكهف، صفحة ٣٠٢» and opens page 302.
3. List mode: open al-Kahf in the ayah list, scroll to about ayah 45 and stop; go back to Today; the tile reads «… الآية ٤٥» and opens the list with ayah 45 at the top. Open another surah: the tile names it without an ayah until you scroll it.
4. Qada: with every prayer owed, «قضيتُ يوماً كاملاً» lowers each of the five by one and leaves the fast; «تراجع عن الأخير» once restores all five. With one prayer at zero the button is disabled.
5. Widget picker (long-press the home screen, Widgets, Wird): Android 12+ shows the widget with the sample text; an Android 8–11 phone shows the drawn card. Adding the widget still shows the real next prayer.
6. Open the app on another tab or a pushed screen (for example the Mafatih reader), then tap a reminder notification: the app shows «اليوم». Tap the playing adhan's notification: the adhan stops and the app shows «اليوم».
7. Turn on «تذكير مسائي قبل الليالي المباركة» on a Thursday before Maghrib: a notification «ليلة الجمعة» comes an hour before Maghrib. Turn it off: no notification the next Thursday. Reboot with it on: it still comes.
8. Turn on «رفع صوت الأذان تدريجياً» and press «تجربة»: the adhan starts quietly and reaches full volume in about 10 seconds; the phone's alarm volume slider does not move. Stop it in the first seconds by each stop path (notification, power key, banner): it stops. With the switch off, the adhan starts at full volume.
9. Export a backup, change the place, the theme, a favourite and the morning reminder, then import the backup: without restarting, the place, theme, favourites and the reminder on Today show the imported values, and a tap does not bring the changed values back. The message has no restart request.
