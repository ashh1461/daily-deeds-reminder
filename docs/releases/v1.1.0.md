# Daily Deeds Reminder 1.1.0

Adds five readings supplied in the reference images, organized by their stated times:

- **Anytime:** the short ziyara of Sahib al-Zaman, including the instruction to place a hand over the heart.
- **Anytime:** the supplication entrusting one's future to Allah, with a three-repetition counter.
- **Before sleep:** Quran 16:6, “ولكم فيها جمال حين تريحون وحين تسرحون”, with a three-repetition counter.
- **At night:** “اللهم يا مغير الأحوال غير حالي إلى أحسن حال”, without assigning an unsupported repetition count.
- **Thursday morning:** the supplied reading instructions for leaving home to seek a need: the end of Aal Imran, Ayat al-Kursi, Al-Qadr, and Al-Fatiha. The source image does not identify the verse range for the end of Aal Imran, so the app preserves that wording.

Night, bedtime, and Thursday reminders default to 21:00, 22:00, and Thursday 07:00 in the device's time zone. Each can be edited or disabled. These are suggested reminder times, not clock times stated in the images. The first two texts have no assigned reminder because their images specify no time.

Thursday readings count toward daily progress only on Thursdays; their category remains accessible throughout the week. Notification taps open the related category. Reminders are rescheduled after reboot, app updates, and time or time-zone changes. Each firing reschedules only itself so reminders at the same time remain independent.

Existing deed IDs, saved progress, and morning/evening reminder preferences are preserved. Date changes refresh daily progress and counter actions check the date before saving.
