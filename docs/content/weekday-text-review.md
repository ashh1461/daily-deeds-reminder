## Transcription and edition notes

The printed scan controls these changes. Online transcriptions from [Alseraj, Friday dua](https://alseraj.net/مفاتيح_الجنان/دُعاء-يوم-الجمعة/) and [Rafed, Sunday Fatima ziyarat](https://research.rafed.net/الدعاء-والزيارة/381-الزيارات/زيارة-النّبيّ-وَأهل-بيته-ع-في-أيّام-الأسْبُوع/1500-زيارةُ-الزّهْرَاءِ-ع-في-يوم-الأحد) assisted transcription and comparison.

In Fatima's first narration, the scan prints «ظاهر بولايتك». Rafed prints «طاهر» and records «ظاهر» as a variant. The app follows the scanned edition and retains «ظاهر»; the regression test makes this editorial choice explicit.

All fourteen main weekday entries were visually compared against the sections listed above for wording and missing passages. This is not a certification of every diacritic or of agreement between editions. Modern punctuation, paragraph breaks, and ordinary orthographic normalization remain. Optional surrounding prose, Saturday's alternative ziyarat, and the poem following Friday's ziyarat are outside the main entries reviewed here.

Regression tests cover the completed Friday ending, both Sunday Fatima narrations, excerpt flags, source page labels, and the corrected Sunday preposition.
