# Wird 1.8.1.6: small fixes, including a backup regression from 1.8.1.5 (pre-release)

Builds on 1.8.1.5 and installs over it and earlier 1.8.x builds (same signing key, version code 19). It fixes the items found in the second review round (`docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md` section 7.5).

## Fixes
- **Restoring a backup kept the last mushaf page and the Quran view again.** In 1.8.1.5 those two settings were written to the backup but rejected on restore as "invalid items", and because a restore clears the current values first, they were lost even on the same phone. A backup made with 1.8.1.5 already contains them, so restoring it with 1.8.1.6 brings them back.
- **A weekday du'a or ziyarah opened from the favourites, or from search, now shows that day.** Before, a starred «دعاء يوم الأحد» opened on a Tuesday showed Tuesday's du'a.
- **«تصغير الخط» in the mushaf never makes the text bigger.** Fitted pages could use 12 sp while the zoom stopped at 14 sp, so the first "smaller" step from a dense page enlarged it. Both now share one minimum.
- **«تراجع» in the favourites only puts back what was deleted.** Before, a star added elsewhere after the delete (for example in the mushaf) was removed again by the undo.
- **«تراجع عن الأخير» in qada undoes a whole "add days" at once.** Adding days of prayers writes one change per prayer; the undo used to revert only the last of the five.
- **Reader text sizes are kept between launches**: the ayah list, Mafatih and the mushaf size, and whether mushaf pages fit the screen. They are also part of a backup.
- **The basmala's line height grows with its text**, so a basmala that wraps onto two lines at a large size no longer overlaps itself.
- **Privacy policy:** «موقعي» now asks the phone for a current location (up to 10 seconds) and falls back to the last known one; `docs/PRIVACY.md` says so.

## Compatibility
- Backups gain four settings: `pref_font_quran`, `pref_font_mafatih`, `pref_font_mushaf` and `pref_mushaf_fit`. Older versions skip them when restoring; everything else restores as before.
- Qada history written by earlier versions still reads; each old entry undoes on its own, as before.
- Reader sizes restored from a backup show after the app is next started, like the other settings that screens already hold in memory (reloading after an import is planned for v1.8.3).

## Verification
- New unit tests:
  - the backup keeps the Int and Boolean reader settings, and rejects them with a wrong type
  - the weekday route carries the day, ignores a bad day, and search results carry their day
  - the mushaf zoom never goes below the fit minimum, and stepping down from it does not enlarge the text
  - the favourites undo keeps a star added after the delete
  - undoing a bulk qada add reverts all five prayers, and older history still decodes
  - stored reader sizes are clamped to each reader's range
  - the basmala line height scales with its size
- Full unit test run, Android lint and the debug build: see `progress.md`.

## Not verified (needs a person with a phone)
1. Make a backup with 1.8.1.5 in pages mode on a page other than 1, install 1.8.1.6, restore it: the Quran tab resumes on that page.
2. Star «دعاء يوم الأحد» and «زيارة يوم الأحد», then open them from the favourites on another day: Sunday's text shows. Do the same from search.
3. On a dense page (for example page 48), with fit on, tap «تصغير الخط»: the text does not grow.
4. Delete a favourite, star an ayah in the mushaf, come back, tap «تراجع»: both are starred.
5. Qada: add 10 days of prayers, then «تراجع عن الأخير»: all five go back.
6. Change the text size in the ayah list, Mafatih and the mushaf, and turn fit off; close the app fully and reopen: the sizes and the fit choice are kept.
7. At the largest mushaf size, a surah start (for example page 50, Al Imran): the basmala does not overlap.
