# Daily Deeds Reminder 1.3.0

Feature release based on a comparison with other Shia apps (Shia Toolkit, Shia Muslim, Mafatih readers): adds the tools they offer that Daily Deeds was missing. Everything works offline.

## New
- **Jafari prayer times:** Fajr 16°, Maghrib 4° after sunset, Isha 14°, plus sunrise, sunset and legal midnight (halfway between sunset and next Fajr). Choose one of 28 preset cities (Beirut, Najaf, Karbala, Qom, Mashhad, Kuwait, Qatif, London, Dearborn...) or tap «موقعي» to use the device's last known location (coarse location permission, asked only on tap). Optional adhan reminders per prayer.
- **Qibla compass:** great-circle bearing to the Kaaba for the selected place, with a live arrow that uses the rotation sensor and magnetic declination; shows the bearing in degrees when there is no compass sensor.
- **Calendar and occasions:** Hijri date with a −2..+2 day moon-sighting adjustment, and 42 occasions of the Ahl al-Bayt (births, martyrdoms, Eids, Laylat al-Qadr nights) with a countdown and an optional 8:00 reminder on the day. Some dates differ between sources; the commonly observed one is shown with a note to check your marja.
- **Favorites:** star any ayah, dua/ziyarat from Mafatih (including the full book) or weekday dua/ziyarat; a Favorites screen lists them.
- **Continue where you left off:** the Home tab remembers the last surah and last Mafatih item.
- **Sahifa Sajjadiyya:** a link in the Mafatih tab opens the Al-Maaref online edition in the browser (not bundled; see limits).

## Accuracy
- Prayer times were checked against an independent Jafari reference (aladhan.com, method 0) for Beirut, Baghdad, Qom, London and Dearborn on 2026-10-15: every time within 2 minutes (automated test). Qibla bearings were checked against the same source within 0.3°.
- Always compare with the timetable of your own marja or local office, especially near sunrise/sunset and in high latitudes (the app shows "—" when a time cannot be computed).

## Known limits
- The Sahifa Sajjadiyya is not bundled: the Al-Maaref PDF is a scanned image edition marked "all rights reserved to the publisher", and no openly licensed digital text was found.
- Hijri dates use Umm al-Qura tables; adjust the offset to match local sighting.
- Qibla compass and "my location" were not tested on a real device in the build environment.
- Debug-signed APK.
