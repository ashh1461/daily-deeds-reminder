# Wird 1.8.1.2: two bundled adhan recordings (pre-release)

The app now plays a **real adhan** at prayer time. Until now it bundled no adhan audio: with no voice installed it played the phone's notification sound. This release also carries all the fixes of 1.8.1.1. It installs over 1.8.1 and 1.8.1.1 (same signing key, version code 15).

**This is published as a pre-release** because the new audio has not been played on a physical phone yet (see "Not verified on a device"). It is not marked Latest.

## The adhan
- **Two built-in recordings:** «أذان الحرم الحسيني – كربلاء المقدسة» (the default) and «أذان الحرم الرضوي – مشهد المقدسة». Each is about 5 minutes and plays to its last phrase. Choose one per prayer in Prayer settings → voice, or pick «نغمة الهاتف» (the phone's sound) or your own audio file.
- **Behaviour change you should know about:** anyone who had the old option «نغمة الهاتف الافتراضية» now hears the Karbala adhan, at alarm volume, for about 5 minutes (also at Fajr). To keep the old sound, choose «نغمة الهاتف» in Prayer settings.
- **The «تجربة» (test) button** plays only the first 20 seconds.
- **It yields to calls and other apps:** if another app takes audio focus (a call, another alarm) the adhan stops and the usual prayer-time notification is left behind.
- **If the audio cannot play on a device** (for example a codec problem) before it starts, the app falls back once to the phone's sound, so a prayer time is never silent.
- **Size:** the APK grows from about 9.8 MB to 15.5 MB. The recordings are stored as Ogg Opus (about 2.8 MB each).

## Where the recordings come from (read this)
Both recordings were supplied by the project owner, who states that they are free of licence restrictions. No licence text, source link or reciter's name accompanies the files (they are MP3s converted from online videos), so the rights were **not independently verified** and the reciters are not credited because the files do not identify them. If you hold rights in either recording and object, open an issue and the file will be removed. Details, processing steps and hashes: `docs/licenses/ADHAN_AUDIO.md`; credit text: Settings → About. The owner must confirm the rights before any store publication.

Processing: silence trimmed at one end of each file (first 1.4 s of the Karbala file; the Mashhad file cut at 310.6 s where its silence begins), static gain to -14 LUFS with a peak limiter (the sources were at -7 and -9 LUFS and one clipped), a short fade-in and fade-out, Ogg Opus 80 kbps. Nothing else was changed.

## Also in this release (from 1.8.1.1)
Imsak no longer lost when it falls on the same minute as the Fajr pre-alert; late alerts no longer claim "now"; a silent phone still gets a notification; a Hijri-offset change replans the alarms; reminder taps open their section; the Mafatih counter survives rotation; al-Mizan is copied only when first opened; the boot receiver is private and app data is excluded from device transfer. Full list: `docs/releases/v1.8.1.1.md`.

## Verification
- Unit tests pass, including 7 new ones that pin the size and SHA-256 of both files, check that they are Ogg Opus and that the declared length covers the real length, and cover the voice-id rules. Android lint 0 errors (17 warnings, the recorded baseline). The release APK is the signed, minified build, checked with `scripts/verify_release.py`, and both `.ogg` files were confirmed stored uncompressed inside it (needed to play them from the APK).
- Played on an Android 14 emulator (API 34) with this exact signed APK: the Karbala adhan (the default) and, after choosing it in the picker, the Mashhad adhan each started in a `MediaPlayer` on the alarm stream and kept playing (checked at 3, 8 and 14 seconds), with no errors or crashes in the log. The «تجربة» button stopped itself after about 20 seconds and the service ended. The voice menu showed the two adhan recordings and «نغمة الهاتف», and the Arabic text rendered right-to-left. This proves the files decode and play from the APK; it does not prove how they sound.
- Not listened to during the build: the audio was checked structurally (about 18 to 19 evenly spaced phrases per file, consistent with one complete adhan each), not by ear, so the words and the exact start and end were not confirmed. Please listen to both once.

## Not verified (needs a person with a phone)
Please check on a phone and tell the maintainer if any of these misbehave:
- Both adhan recordings sound right: they start at the right place, are not cut off, and end with the last phrase (the emulator run showed only that they play; nobody has listened to them).
- A full 5-minute playback to the end on a real phone, and that the adhan stops when a call comes in.
- Stop on the notification works; choosing «نغمة الهاتف» plays the phone's sound; the other prayers keep their own choice.
- The new adhan credit at the end of Settings → About reads correctly right-to-left.
- Everything listed under "Not verified on a device" in `docs/releases/v1.8.1.1.md`.
