# Wird 1.9.0: the app in Arabic, English and Persian (pre-release)

The app's own interface can now be shown in **Arabic, English or Persian**, or follow the phone's language. Arabic stays the default, so nothing changes for existing users until they pick another language in Settings. It installs over 1.8.x with the same signing key (version code 24), and your settings, favourites, reading positions and backups carry over unchanged.

## What changed

- **A Language section in Settings.** Choose العربية, English, فارسی, or "follow the device". Each language is named in its own script. The app switches at once; no restart is needed.
- **The whole interface follows the choice:**
  - all screens, menus, dialogs and messages;
  - prayer, adhan, deed-reminder, morning-note and blessed-eve notifications, and the notification channel names in Android settings;
  - the home-screen widget;
  - the timetable PDF headers;
  - Hijri months and dates, the 49 occasions of the Ahl al-Bayt, weekday names and the 28 suggested cities.
- **Layout direction follows the language:** right to left in Arabic and Persian, left to right in English.
- **Religious text is never translated.** The Quran, supplications, ziyarat, Mafatih al-Jinan, al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and al-Mizan stay in Arabic and are always shown right to left, whatever the interface language. So do the deed titles and instructions, and the Salat al-Ayat rulings.
- The launcher name stays «ورد» in every language.

## Compatibility

- Existing users stay in Arabic. The language choice is a new setting, saved in the storage that is readable before the first unlock, so a locked-phone adhan notification is written in the chosen language.
- A city saved before you change the language keeps the name it was saved with; the 28 suggested cities are shown in the new language.
- Backups are unchanged: the language choice is not included in them.
- No new permissions. No change to what the app sends or stores on any server (it still has no backend and no account).
- App bundles keep every language (language splits are off), so a phone set to one language can still switch to the others.

## Known limits

- **The Persian text is a draft** and needs a native speaker's check before this becomes a Latest release.
- Persian city search shows Arabic city names (the offline city list has no Persian names).
- Mafatih category names, the mushaf's TalkBack action label and the surah name in the al-Mizan surah screen and the ayah dialog stay Arabic.
- Persian uses Arabic-Indic digits (٤٥٦) in the interface, not Persian ones (۴۵۶), except in dates and notifications.
- English occasion titles use "(PBUH)" for the Prophet; this is a style choice that can be changed.

## Verification

- `StringResourcesTest` keeps the Arabic, English and Persian string files in step: the same keys and placeholders, nothing empty, no Arabic script in English.
- New unit tests: `AppLanguageTest` (choosing and resolving the language), `AlarmWordsTest` (prayer notification wording per language, and that its prayer names match the string resources), `NoteWordsTest` (morning notes and blessed eves), `CalendarNamesTest` (Arabic output exactly as before; every occasion and suggested city has an English and a Persian name), `PlaceSearchLatinTest` (English city names in the English interface).
- Changed tests: the countdown, coordinate input, backup import and Quran resume now return data that the interface words; their tests assert that data.
- Full unit-test run, Android lint and the debug build: 356 unit tests, 0 failures; lint 0 errors.
- Each of the seven stages was reviewed by a separate read-only Kotlin review before it was committed; every finding was fixed.

## Not verified (needs a person with a phone)

1. **Install over 1.8.5:** the app opens in Arabic exactly as before, and settings, the place, favourites and reading positions are all still there.
2. **Switch to English:** Settings › Language › English. Every tab reads left to right in English: Today, prayer times, the tools, the readers' menus and the calendar.
3. **Religious text stays Arabic in English:** open a surah, a Mafatih supplication, the Sahifa and an al-Mizan page; the text is Arabic, right to left, and lines up as before.
4. **Notifications in English:** with English chosen, let a prayer alarm (or a pre-prayer alert) fire and check a deed reminder; both are in English. Android Settings › Apps › Wird › Notifications shows English channel names.
5. **Locked phone:** restart the phone with English chosen and leave it locked; the adhan notification at the next prayer is in English.
6. **Widget:** the widget shows the next prayer, the Hijri date, the occasion and the city in English, and in Arabic again after switching back.
7. **Persian:** switch to فارسی; screens read right to left in Persian. Ask a Persian speaker to read through the main screens and notifications.
8. **Follow the device:** choose "follow the device", set the phone to English, then to another language such as French; the app shows English, then Arabic (the fallback).
9. **City names:** in English, the suggested cities and the city search show English names; "My location" reads "My current location (near …)".
10. **Switch back to Arabic:** everything, including the widget and the notification channels, returns to Arabic.
11. **Backup:** export a backup in English and import it; every value round-trips as before. The language choice itself is not part of the backup, so the app stays in the language it is set to.
