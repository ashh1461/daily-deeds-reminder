# Daily Deeds Reminder 1.7.0

Worship tools: tasbih, make-up prayers, Ramadan timetable, Salat al-Ayat, khums, a home-screen widget and backup. All of them are cards in the العبادات tab.

## Tasbih (المسبحة)
- **Tasbih al-Zahra (peace be upon her):** 34 takbir, 33 tahmid, 33 tasbih with the current stage highlighted, a completion count and haptic feedback (follows the existing switch). The stage rule is shared with the daily-deed counter, so both always agree.
- **Free counter** with an optional target (33, 34, 99, 100, 313, 1000 or open) and a lifetime total.

## Make-up prayers and fasts (القضاء)
- Counters for the five daily prayers and for fasts; "I made one up" lowers the count and can never go below zero.
- Add one, add a number, or add N days to all five prayers at once. The last eight changes are listed and the latest one can be undone.
- Stored on the device only.

## Ramadan and Imsak timetable (رمضان والإمساك)
- Monthly table for any Hijri month (Ramadan by default): Imsak, Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Maghrib, Isha. Today's row is highlighted; the days whose Maghrib starts the nights of Qadr (19, 21, 23) are marked in gold.
- Uses the city, calculation method, manual offsets and Hijri-day adjustment from the prayer settings.
- **PDF export** (one A4 page) through the system file picker.
- **Imsak alarm** on the days of Ramadan, independent of the Fajr alarm. For Iftar, enable the Maghrib adhan or its reminder.
- Fix: a manual Fajr offset now moves Imsak too.

## Salat al-Ayat (صلاة الآيات)
- A short guide: when it is due, its time, the two ways to perform it and the recommended acts. It is a summary of the commonly circulated practical rulings and says to check one's own marja.
- **Eclipse calendar 2021-2040** from the NASA/GSFC eclipse catalogue (F. Espenak). For lunar eclipses the app computes whether the Moon is above the horizon in the chosen city at the moment of greatest eclipse; solar eclipses show their regions and ask to verify locally.
- Optional 08:00 notification when a visible lunar eclipse (not a penumbral one, which is not noticeable and needs no prayer) falls within the next 24 hours.

## Khums calculator (حاسبة الخمس)
- Gains minus living expenses minus debts gives the surplus; khums is 20% of it, minus what was already paid, split into the share of the Imam and the share of the sayyids. Arabic or Western digits and separators are accepted.
- The date of the khums year (Hijri day and month) with the next Gregorian date and an optional reminder a week before and on the day.
- The screen states that it is a general-rule estimate and not a fatwa, and nothing is paid through the app.

## Widget
- Home-screen widget with the city, the next prayer and its time, the Hijri date and the occasion of the day. It is redrawn at each prayer and at midnight with an inexact alarm (no extra permission) and on every settings change.

## Backup
- Settings → «البيانات والنسخ الاحتياطي»: export favorites, reading positions, deed history, tasbih, make-up counters, khums year, prayer, adhan and reminder settings to a JSON file, and import it on another phone (the app then asks to restart). Voice files are not included, and a backed-up voice that is missing falls back to the default.
- A backup file is treated as untrusted input: only known keys with the right types are accepted, the place is validated as a whole (a bad latitude or time zone is dropped), and size is capped. The stored place is also validated when it is read.

## Under the hood
- The morning alarm now serves three notes (occasion, khums year, lunar eclipse) through a pure, tested planner; the prayer planner gained an Imsak alarm kind.
- No new permission and no new dependency. The widget receiver is not exported. See `docs/SECURITY.md`.

## Known limits
- Not tested on a device or emulator: widget placement and refresh, PDF rendering of Arabic text, the file pickers, the restart after import, and notification delivery.
- The Moon formula is accurate to about 0.3 degrees: a lunar eclipse whose greatest phase happens within a few minutes of moonrise or moonset can be reported on the wrong side. Delta-T is taken as 70 seconds.
- Solar-eclipse visibility is not computed.
- Fiqh details (khums deductions, the time limit of Salat al-Ayat) differ between maraji and are only summarised.
